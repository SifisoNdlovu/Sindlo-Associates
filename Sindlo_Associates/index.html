Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our legal services and processes.

What types of legal cases do you handle?

We handle a wide range of legal matters including fire accidents, accident injuries, family law, financial law, drug offences, and civil litigation. Our experienced team of attorneys specializes in various practice areas to provide comprehensive legal representation.

How do I schedule a consultation?

You can schedule a free consultation by calling us at (0761) 654-123987, emailing us at hello@sindloassociate.com, or clicking the "Free Consultant" button on our website. We're available Monday through Sunday from 09:00 to 17:00.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

For your first consultation, please bring any relevant documents related to your case, such as contracts, correspondence, medical records (if applicable), police reports, and any other documentation that may be relevant to your legal matter.

How much do your legal services cost?

Our fees vary depending on the complexity and type of case. We offer transparent pricing and will discuss all fees and payment options during your initial consultation. We also work on a contingency basis for certain types of cases.

How long does a typical case take to resolve?

The duration of a case depends on various factors including the complexity of the matter, court schedules, and the cooperation of all parties involved. During your consultation, we'll provide an estimated timeline based on your specific situation.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand that legal services can be a significant investment. We offer flexible payment plans to accommodate our clients' financial situations. Please discuss payment options with us during your consultation.

Will my case go to trial?

Not all cases go to trial. Many legal matters are resolved through negotiation, mediation, or settlement. However, if your case requires litigation, our experienced trial attorneys are prepared to represent you in court and fight for your rights.

What makes Sindlo & Associates different from other law firms?

At Sindlo & Associates, we combine decades of legal experience with a client-centered approach. We have successfully handled over 152 cases, closed 312 cases, and served 513 trusted clients. Our team of 25 expert attorneys is dedicated to providing personalized attention and achieving the best possible outcomes for each client.
